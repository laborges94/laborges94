### 🚀 About Me

With nearly a decade of experience in software engineering, I specialize in designing scalable systems with a strong commitment to **Clean Architecture**, **Domain-Driven Design (DDD)**, and **Software Quality Engineering**.

Beyond writing robust backend and full-stack code, I am deeply invested in developer experience (DevEx) — optimizing team workflows, establishing CI/CD automation, and championing maintainable engineering practices. I value craftsmanship and lightweight, purposeful solutions over unnecessary library bloat (often crafting custom in-house extensions and mediators to keep dependencies clean).

- 🎓 **Education:** Degree in Systems Analysis & Development (IFSP) | Postgraduate in Software Quality Engineering (SENAC).
- 💡 **Philosophy:** Keep software maintainable, well-tested, and purposeful. The best solutions solve complex domain problems with clarity and minimal friction.
- 🤝 **Collaboration:** Enthusiastic about open-source tools, developer productivity, and AI-accelerated workflows.
